Real-estate developer, Sarabha Nagar, Ludhiana (Project 59-D commercial, Gurmeet Tower). Positioning: credible, premium, investor-facing ("smart investors ki pehli choice").

## Design system

- **Metal:** warm gold. Tokens: `gold oklch(0.8 0.125 80)`, `goldDeep oklch(0.64 0.12 68)`, glow `gold / 0.4`. Bevel face `#dfb25a`, extrusion `#8f6726 → #4a3312`.
- **Ground:** warm near-black `oklch(0.135 0.008 70)` with radial lift, gold dust, grain 0.08, vignette.
- **Ink:** ivory `oklch(0.96 0.012 85)`, dim `oklch(0.74 0.012 85)`. Crimson `oklch(0.55 0.2 25)` exists for one slam per reel only.
- **Type:** Geist (display 640 / light 300 / body 450); Archivo wdth 125 tracked caps for labels — echoes the wide wordmark.
- **Logo:** DHUNNA / INFRA wide geometric wordmark. Use the white master (`dhunna-infra-wordmark-white.png`) rebuilt in gold by `GoldLogo` (saber edge trace → metal fill → sheen). The old "INFRA HOLDINGS" gold PNG is superseded.
- **3D icon set** (Three.js, gold / ivory / ink clay): map pin on a city tile, hard hat, studded shield with swinging padlock, rocket, pinned note card, ₹ coins.
